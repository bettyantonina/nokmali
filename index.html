About me

Hi, I'm Helene.

I'm a health, nutrition and life rhythm coach, a yoga teacher, a mother, and an engineer.

I enjoy looking at things from different angles, asking good questions, and finding simple ways through complex situations. After more than fifteen years of living and working internationally, I know how much lives can move, stretch, and change.

Health, habits, creativity, body awareness, and a workable everyday rhythm are part of my own life. Painting, yoga, and time in nature are some of the ways I stay sane and connected.

In coaching, we explore your habits, body signals, priorities, energy, and daily rhythms. We look at what is already there, what matters most to you, and what small changes could support you in everyday life.

I don’t tell you who to become. I help you hear yourself more clearly through listening, meaningful questions, and practical support.

Good to know.

Coaching is a collaborative, thought-provoking process, not a legal business partnership. You drive each session and choose what you want to work towards. I support you with focused attention, reflection, honest feedback, professional care, and respect for your right to make your own decisions.

You are responsible for your well-being, choices, actions, and results. You agree to participate fully, communicate honestly, remain open to feedback, and speak up if the coaching is not working for you. Results cannot be guaranteed and depend on your commitment to taking action. Please arrive on time and give at least 24 hours’ notice when cancelling or rescheduling; late cancellations may incur a fee.

Coaching is not therapy and does not diagnose, prevent, cure, or treat mental disorders or medical conditions. It does not replace medical, mental health, legal, or other qualified professional advice. When appropriate, I may recommend that you consult another professional. If you are already receiving professional care, you are encouraged to tell that provider about our coaching work.

Our conversations and records are kept private and securely stored in accordance with applicable privacy law and professional duty of care. Your information or name will not be shared without your consent. With your permission, your name and contact details may be kept in a coaching log, but no session information will be included. Coaching confidentiality is not legally privileged in the same way as medicine or law.
