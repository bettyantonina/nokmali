Nokmali Coaching

Tools

Simple self-reflection tools for energy, decisions, and mixed feelings.

These tools can help you slow down, organize your thoughts, and see your situation from more than one angle. You can use them before a coaching session, after a session, or on your own when you want more clarity.

There are no right answers. Use what is useful and leave the rest.

Energy and daily rhythm

Energy Rebalance

Look at where your energy is going, what gives energy back, and what may need adjusting in your day-to-day life.

Open Energy Rebalance

Choices and change

Decisional Balance

Explore both sides of a decision: what keeps you where you are, what supports change, and what the trade-offs may be.

Open Decisional Balance

Mixed feelings

Parts of Me

Map different parts of yourself that may want different things, so you can understand the tension instead of forcing a quick answer.

Open Parts of Me

When to use these tools

Use them when you feel stuck, unsure, scattered, overloaded, or when you want to prepare for a coaching conversation.

  • Get thoughts out of your head
  • Compare options more clearly
  • Notice what needs attention next
